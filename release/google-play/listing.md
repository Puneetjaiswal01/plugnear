## Full description
PlugNear is an India-first EV charging platform in early access, bringing drivers, charger owners and businesses together.

Built for cars, taxis, bikes, scooters, three-wheelers and commercial fleets, PlugNear is developing two simple journeys: find a nearby charger and list a charging space.

This early-access version lets you explore the app and email registration experience. Google and phone sign-in depend on provider availability and may be unavailable while setup is completed. Hosting, nearby discovery and booking workflows remain under development and require backend verification before launch.

A separate local fleet planning tool is available on www.plugnear.in. It provides illustrative energy and charging-window estimates, not a charging reservation or engineering design.

Live charging bookings and online payments are not yet available. Charger compatibility and site access must be checked individually. Do not pay hosts outside the platform.

Location is requested only while using location-based features. A contact phone saved in your profile is separate from an OTP-verified login number.

Visit www.plugnear.in to learn about the platform and its current availability.
