A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Getting connected should feel simple.
Here’s a place to start.

What exactly is PlugNear?+

PlugNear is building an India-first charging marketplace for cars, taxis, bikes, scooters, three-wheelers and commercial fleets. The same platform brings together charger hosts, homes, societies, shops, hotels, workplaces, parking facilities and depots. Each vehicle and site needs compatible equipment and reviewed access. See the Roadmap for future services.

Can I book and pay for a charge today?+

The pilot supports requests to verified hosts and direct UPI checkout after host acceptance. Payment is reported by the driver and manually confirmed by the host; it is not bank-verified by PlugNear. Availability depends on approved hosts. Email sign-up is available; you do not need phone OTP to create an account.

Who can become a charging host?+

Homes, shops, hotels, workplaces, parking operators and fleet depots can be considered as hosts. Sites need hosting permission, suitable parking, reviewed electrical supply, approved equipment and a vehicle compatibility declaration. Submit your charger through the app for review. Host verification is required before a listing can be approved.

Will a charger work with my EV?+

Cars, taxis, bikes, three-wheelers and larger commercial vehicles have different charging requirements. A vehicle category or listing alone does not establish compatibility. Never assume a generic socket or adapter is suitable. Check the listed connector against your manufacturer’s instructions. Read our compatibility checklist.

Will PlugNear support UPI?+

The pilot provides a direct UPI link and QR for a confirmed booking. Always check the displayed recipient and amount in your UPI app. The host checks receipt manually; PlugNear does not hold funds, verify the bank transfer or provide automatic refunds.

Is there an Android or iPhone app?+

Use the installable web app now. On iPhone, open Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option. Native store releases are listed in the Roadmap.

HOST PRICING, EXPLAINED

Understand the rate before you host.

Hosts set a price per kWh. The pilot calculates the booking total from the requested energy estimate and the listed rate. For example, 5 kWh at ₹15 per kWh is ₹75 gross, before electricity costs, site costs and any applicable taxes. This is an example, not a forecast of earnings.

The pilot does not independently meter delivered energy or automatically reconcile a session against a meter. Agree the charging estimate and access instructions before starting. Direct UPI goes to the host; refunds and disputes require manual resolution through the host and support.
