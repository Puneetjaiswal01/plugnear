A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Getting connected should feel simple.
Here’s a place to start.

What exactly is PlugNear?+

PlugNear is building an India-first neighbourhood charging network for electric two- and three-wheelers. The first proposed supply is local micro-hosts; fleet pilots are the next audience. We are not claiming an integrated national CPO network.

Can I book and pay for a charge today?+

Not yet. The platform is in early access while booking workflows, payment verification and launch checks are completed. You can explore the app and its registration experience. Do not send money to hosts outside the platform.

Can a shop or parking operator become a micro-host?+

Micro-host onboarding is being designed. Sites will need hosting permission, suitable parking, reviewed electrical supply, approved equipment and a vehicle compatibility declaration. The current early-access listing form is a charger-registration prototype; socket-based micro-host listings are not enabled yet.

Will a charger work with my EV?+

Two- and three-wheelers have different OEM charger and supply requirements; a Type 2 or CCS2 listing does not establish compatibility. Never assume a generic socket or adapter is suitable. The current app supports its listed connector types; 2W/3W compatibility-aware discovery is still planned.

Will PlugNear support UPI?+

India-friendly payments, including UPI, are part of the plan. Payment services are not enabled yet. Supported methods will be shown only after payment-provider setup and verification are complete.

Is there an Android or iPhone app?+

Android and iOS apps are being developed with Expo. They have not been released on Google Play or the Apple App Store. The early-access web app is available from this website.
