BEFORE BOOKING CAN OPEN

Local readiness
comes first.

  • Business and operating approvals. Confirm the legal entity, trade activity and applicable DEWA requirements with qualified UAE advisers and authorities.
  • AED payments and refunds. A suitable UAE merchant/provider setup, reconciliation and refund process are needed. India’s INR/UPI checkout is not a Dubai payment service.
  • Local time and support. Dubai planning uses Asia/Dubai (UTC+4). UAE contact, English/Arabic support and approved local terms need preparation.
  • Real equipment and acceptance tests. Check vehicle/site compatibility and approved installations, then run a supervised local charging/payment test.
