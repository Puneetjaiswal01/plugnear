Start with your vehicle’s charging inlet.

Look in your vehicle manual or ask the manufacturer which connector, supply and charging equipment it supports. PlugNear’s current filters cover Type 2, CCS2, Bharat AC and CHAdeMO; this is not a vehicle-model compatibility database.

  • Type 2: an AC connector. The vehicle’s onboard charger and site supply can limit the rate below the charger’s advertised power.
  • CCS2: DC charging for vehicles fitted and approved for that inlet. A Type 2 AC inlet alone does not establish CCS2 support.
  • CHAdeMO: a separate DC standard. Check the exact inlet and manufacturer guidance.
  • Bharat AC: check the actual outlet and your manufacturer-approved portable charging equipment with the host. This label does not mean any household socket is suitable.

Electric bikes and three-wheelers may use proprietary chargers or removable batteries. Do not assume car connectors fit them. Do not improvise adapters or extension leads; use manufacturer-approved equipment and a reviewed electrical installation.

Open charger discovery

A map pin is the start of a conversation.

The app shows approved listings from verified pilot hosts. This review is not an electrical safety guarantee, insurance cover or a promise that every vehicle will fit.

  • Available: the current host/booking status allows a request. A suitable host-published window is still required.
  • Busy: a booking currently occupies the charger.
  • Offline: the host has made the charger unavailable.

These are app records, not live electrical telemetry. Open the listing, select a window and review the address and access instructions. Ask about parking, gates and vehicle size. A request needs host acceptance; do not travel or pay beforehand.

Map distance is straight-line distance. Directions open an external navigation service; road distance and travel time can differ. Sorting and Available now only refine up to 50 results returned for a search. Try another area or wider radius if no approved listing matches.

Understand the estimate before you request.

The pilot estimate is your requested kWh multiplied by the listed ₹/kWh rate. For example, 5 kWh at ₹20/kWh gives a ₹100 estimate. These numbers illustrate the calculation, not an available offer or a regulated tariff recommendation.

Energy divided by power is only an idealised time estimate. Actual charging can be slower because of vehicle limits, battery state, temperature, power sharing and losses. The app’s reserved energy is not an independently metered bill.

After host acceptance, check the booking’s UPI recipient and amount. Pay the host directly, report the transfer and wait for manual host confirmation. PlugNear does not bank-verify the receipt, hold funds or automatically refund payments. Never share a UPI PIN or OTP with the host or support.

Read cancellation, refund and dispute terms

Better charging starts with useful feedback.

If equipment looks damaged or unsafe, stop and do not use it. For a charging or access problem, contact support with the listing title, booking reference if you have one, what happened and the time. Exclude identity documents, payment credentials and private gate codes.

Public ratings, community photos, check-ins and one-tap issue reports are on the roadmap; no reliability score or testimonials are invented. Support reports are reviewed manually and do not automatically change map status. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
