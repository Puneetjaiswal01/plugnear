Account help

For password resets, open the app, enter your email and select “Forgot password?”. Open the reset link in the same app/browser where you requested it. Google and phone sign-in depend on provider setup. Never share a password, access token, OTP or identity document with support through a public message.

To remove an account, use the account deletion request page. For other issues, include the app/platform, what happened and a screenshot with personal details removed. Do not include passwords, OTPs or payment credentials.

Current availability

PlugNear is in early access. The pilot supports verified-host requests and direct UPI with manual host receipt confirmation. If a transfer is pending or disputed, check your bank/UPI history and contact the host and this support address; do not pay twice. PlugNear does not independently verify transfers or provide automatic refunds. Contacts are not a guaranteed emergency response channel; use local emergency services for an immediate safety issue.
